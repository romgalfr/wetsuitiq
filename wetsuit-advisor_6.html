How to measure your chest

1

Get a soft tape measure. A rigid ruler will not work — use a flexible dressmaker's tape, or a piece of string you can measure against a ruler.

2

Stand up straight, breathe normally. Do not puff out your chest or suck in.

3

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest — this is typically across the nipple line for men. Keep the tape parallel to the floor.

4

Read the measurement. The tape should sit snug but not tight — you should be able to slip one finger underneath.

If you are between centimetres, always round up. For wetsuit sizing, a slightly looser chest is better than a constricting one.
